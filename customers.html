How to work with us

Streamlined access through established government-wide and agency-specific vehicles.

GSA · Government-wide

GSA Schedule 70 (MAS, IT Category)

A long-term, government-wide contract vehicle offering federal, state, and local agencies pre-vetted IT products and services.

U.S. Air Force · IDIQ

SBEAS IDIQ

A preferred procurement vehicle for the Air Force's Business & Enterprise Systems (BES) directorate, also open to other Air Force commands and agencies.

DLA · IDIQ

DLA JETS 2.0

A ten-year, multiple-award, indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract from DLA's J6 directorate, available to other DoD agencies.

Missile Defense Agency

MDA SHIELD

A vehicle for the rapid delivery of innovative capabilities to the warfighter.
